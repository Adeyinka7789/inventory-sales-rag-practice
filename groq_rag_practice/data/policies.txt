ACME INVENTORY & SALES CO. — COMPANY POLICIES

RETURN POLICY
Customers may return unopened products within 14 days of purchase for a full refund.
Opened electronics may be returned within 7 days only if defective, subject to inspection.
Bulk orders (100+ units) are non-returnable unless the entire shipment is defective.
All returns require the original sales receipt or order number.

BULK DISCOUNT POLICY
Orders of 100-499 units receive a 5% discount off the listed unit price.
Orders of 500 units or more receive a 15% discount off the listed unit price.
Discounts apply to unit price only and do not stack with promotional pricing.
Bulk discount requests must be approved by a sales supervisor before invoicing.

SUPPLIER PAYMENT TERMS
Standard supplier payment terms are Net 30 from the invoice date.
Suppliers with a 12+ month relationship qualify for Net 45 terms upon request.
Late supplier payments beyond 60 days incur a 2% monthly penalty.
All supplier invoices must reference a valid purchase order number.

STOCK REORDER POLICY
Products falling below their reorder level trigger an automatic restock alert.
Standard restock lead time is 5-7 business days from approved suppliers.
Fast-moving categories (footwear, safety gear) maintain a 20% safety stock buffer.
Reorder quantities are calculated as: (average weekly sales x 4) minus current stock.

CUSTOMER COMPLAINT ESCALATION
Complaints about product quality are logged and routed to the Quality team within 24 hours.
Complaints involving a refund dispute over ₦50,000 require manager approval.
Repeated complaints (3+) from the same customer trigger an account review.
The assistant must never promise a refund directly — always route to a human for approval.

SHIPPING & DELIVERY POLICY
Standard delivery within Lagos takes 1-2 business days.
Delivery outside Lagos state takes 3-5 business days depending on courier partner.
Orders above ₦200,000 qualify for free delivery; otherwise a flat ₦2,500 fee applies.
Delayed deliveries beyond the quoted window are eligible for a delivery fee refund only, not the order value.
